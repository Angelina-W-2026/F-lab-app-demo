❓ Help & FAQ
What is F Lab?
F Lab is a food science experiment app that helps kids turn food that might go to waste into something delicious, fun, or useful! Each experiment teaches you real science through hands-on cooking.
Are the experiments safe for kids?
Yes! All experiments are designed for ages 5-18 with adult supervision. Each experiment has a safety note. Experiments with heat or sharp tools clearly say to ask an adult for help. Always work with a grown-up when using the stove, oven, or sharp knives.
How does Calendar Sync work?
When you connect your calendar, F Lab looks for birthdays, holidays, and special events. It then suggests food experiments and gift ideas that match those occasions — like a birthday cake for a friend's birthday or candied peel for Mother's Day! Your calendar data stays private and secure.
What do I need to start?
Just basic kitchen tools and ingredients you probably already have — milk, lemons, oranges, soybeans, etc. Each experiment lists exactly what you need. Most experiments use common household items. No fancy equipment required!
How do I level up?
Complete experiments and record them in your Science Journal to earn points. Each experiment gives you points, and challenges give bonus points. Level up to unlock new experiments, badges, and fun features! Level 3 unlocks the "Science Labs" pro section.
Can I do experiments with friends?
Absolutely! Add friends in the "My Lab Friends" section and do challenges together. You can compare how much food you've saved and see who earns the most badges. It's more fun with friends! 🎉
